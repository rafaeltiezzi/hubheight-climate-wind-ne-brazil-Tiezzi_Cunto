# Index data sources
- oni_v6.csv - Oceanic Nino Index v6 (three-month running mean of ERSSTv6 SST anomalies,
  Nino 3.4). NOAA Climate Prediction Center. Accessed 2026-08-22.
  https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso/oni/v6/
- tna_hadisst.txt / tsa_hadisst.txt - Tropical Northern / Southern Atlantic indices
  (SST anomalies, HadISST1.1, climatology 1991-2020). NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory.
  Accessed 2026-09-03. https://psl.noaa.gov/data/timeseries/month/
  Rows: year followed by 12 monthly values; -99.99 = missing.
